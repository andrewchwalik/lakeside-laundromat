About Us

The Lakeside Laundromat

The Lakeside Laundromat is a traditional, coin-operated laundry facility with various sizes of washer and dryers. Our facility is located inside the gates of the Lakeside Chautauqua community, and has been since the 1970s.

We're happy to serve the entire Marblehead Penisula, and beyond, all year round. If you're visiting us in the summer, Lakeside does charge a gate fee, but you can avoid that and do laundry for free - just let the gate attendant know you need a laundry pass.
